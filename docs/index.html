Free and open source · Chrome & Firefox

Your working hours from ARGEPORTAL, at a glance.

PDKS Time Calculator reads your PDKS records when you ask it to and shows daily, weekly and monthly totals, your exit time and what is left for the week. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Works only with ARGEPORTAL portals used by technopark companies.

What it does

Everything you check by hand on the PDKS page, calculated for you in the toolbar popup.

Live daily progress

Time worked today, what remains for your daily target, and your estimated exit time.

Weekly and monthly totals

Totals with a daily cap, plus a summary of the previous week within the current month.

Planning ahead

Plan tomorrow's check-in from a target exit time; on Thursday, plan Friday from the live weekly remainder.

Leave and OOO

Automatic leave detection, manual overrides, and out-of-office time assigned to individual days.

Optional notifications

Get an alert when you reach your daily target or your daily cap, including that day's OOO.

Your rules

Configure your daily target, daily cap, workdays and entry/exit limits. Turkish and English interfaces.

How it works

Set it up once, then refresh whenever you want fresh numbers.

  1. Save your portal addressOpen the extension, go to Settings, and enter your organization's ARGEPORTAL address.
  2. Press RefreshIf ARGEPORTAL isn't open, the extension opens it in a new tab. Sign in if the portal asks you to.
  3. See your summaryOn the portal tab, the extension reads your PDKS records and calculates your totals. They stay available even after you close the portal.

Your data stays in your browser

Attendance records are read only when you refresh, and are stored only in your browser's extension storage. There is no backend, no analytics, and nothing is sold. Read the privacy policy.
